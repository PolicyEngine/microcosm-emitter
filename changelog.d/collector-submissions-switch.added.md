- `HuggingFaceSettings(collector_submissions=True)` switches the Hugging Face module's collector submissions on, and `development_collector_url` points them at a loopback collector, as for telemetry and Orrery graphs. Both are sent only when set, so a build that sets neither starts the emitter as before. A release publication can carry `CollectorProvenance` (`PublishRelease.collector`), which must name its own release and build: once its release commit lands, the emitter submits the build's calibration diagnostics and Orrery inputs from `releases/<release>/<build>`, naming that commit, and keeps copies of the files it submits so the build may remove them after the release lands. A publication Hugging Face refuses is `skipped` with `NOT_PUBLISHED_ON_HUGGING_FACE`. When collector submissions are switched on but the collector settings are refused, `collector_submission_receipt` answers a hand-over that describes its build with a `skipped` receipt and `COLLECTOR_SETTINGS_REFUSED`, instead of nothing (microcosm-emitter#91).
