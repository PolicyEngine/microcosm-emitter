The smaller Ruff rules apply: implicit string concatenations in collections are parenthesized, tests assert one condition at a time and match the messages they expect, and returns, comprehensions, paths and suppressions are written as Ruff requires. Ruff no longer ignores `ISC`, `PT`, `RET`, `ARG`, `PTH`, `E`, `SIM`, `RUF`, `FBT`, `C4`, `PERF`, `PLW` and `A`. Behaviour is unchanged.
