`PromoteBuild` and `WithdrawBuild` take optional `CollectorProvenance` (`collector`), which must name the change's own release and build, as `PublishRelease.collector` does. While collector submissions are switched on, a described promotion or withdrawal is submitted to the collector once its `releases.json` commit lands, as one `releases-record` submission: the `releases.json` at the commit its receipt reports, naming the repository, that commit and the release's current build after the change (for a withdrawal, the current build, not the withdrawn one), with the provenance's country and `licensed_data`; the collector then moves the country's latest calibration tree to follow `releases.json`. A change that leaves the release no current build sends nothing (`skipped`, `NO_CURRENT_BUILD`), and a refused one is `skipped` with `NOT_PUBLISHED_ON_HUGGING_FACE`. The writer keeps the `releases.json` it wrote with the hand-over until the collector row settles, and a change landed by an emitter release that keeps none is `skipped` with `RELEASES_RECORD_MISSING`. Two new protocol fixtures record the described messages (microcosm-emitter#93).
